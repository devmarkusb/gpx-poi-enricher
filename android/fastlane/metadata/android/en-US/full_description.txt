GPX POI Enricher: turn Google Maps directions into a routed GPX track and enrich the route with OpenStreetMap points of interest using built-in YAML profiles (camping, food, beaches, playgrounds, and more).

<b>What you can do</b>
• Paste a Maps directions link and let it be converted to a routed GPX
• Run POI enrichment along a track with configurable profiles (camping, food, beaches, playgrounds, and more)
• Add profiles from a built-in catalog of common OSM POI types (museums, pharmacies, cafés, and more)
• Optionally add evenly spaced split markers
• Edit profiles (YAML)

<b>Who it is for</b>
Campers, hikers, cyclists, and road-trip planners who already use GPX with Garmin, OsmAnd, or Google My Maps and want the enrich / split / maps-to-GPX pipeline on the go.

<b>Recommendation</b>
Use in combination with Maps GPX https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.cismypa.gpxlink&hl=en-US
to view the track on a map and open Google Maps POI information by simple tap.
That way you can selectively choose POIs to be saved in your Google Maps account (Markers, Favorites ...)

<b>Privacy & network</b>
Online steps (geocoding, routing, Overpass) use public endpoints; see the project README for rate limits and attribution. The app does not require an account. It may show third-party ads; you can remove ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase from the project menu.
