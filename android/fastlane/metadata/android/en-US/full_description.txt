GPX POI Enricher: turn Google Maps directions into GPX tracks, then find OpenStreetMap (OSM) points of interest along a track or around a point using built-in YAML profiles.

<b>Tip:</b> Pair with <b>Maps GPX</b> — https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.cismypa.gpxlink — to view tracks on a map and open Google Maps POI details with a tap.

<b>What you can do</b>
• Paste a Maps directions link and let it be converted to a routed GPX
• Search along a GPX track or around coordinates / a Google Maps place link
• Choose a profile (camping, food, beaches, playgrounds, and more)
• Add profiles from a built-in catalog of common OSM POI types (museums, pharmacies, cafés, and more)
• Optionally add evenly spaced split markers
• Edit profiles (YAML)

<b>Who it is for</b>
Campers, hikers, cyclists, and road-trip planners who already use GPX with Garmin, OsmAnd, or Google My Maps and want the enrich / split / maps-to-GPX pipeline on the go.

<b>Maps GPX companion</b>
View enriched tracks on a map, tap POIs for Google Maps details, and save the ones you want to your Google Maps account (Markers, Favorites, …).

<b>Privacy & network</b>
Online steps (geocoding, routing, Overpass) use public endpoints; see the project README for rate limits and attribution. The app does not require an account. It may show third-party ads; you can remove ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase from the project menu.
